Support

Email us: support@justlanded.app
We usually reply within 2 business days.

I can’t sign in

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen, or Email me a code to sign in with a 6-digit code. Codes can take a minute to arrive; check your spam folder.

How do I add a friend?

Open Friends, search for their exact username, and send a request. They see your trips only after they accept, and you see theirs.

Who can see my location?

Only friends you’ve accepted, and only while you have Share live location turned on for a ride. Sharing stops when you turn it off, mark the ride arrived, or check in to your hotel. Landed never tracks you in the background.

My hotel isn’t listed

Hotels come from OpenStreetMap. Tap Can’t find it? Add your hotel to add it for everyone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, trips, check-ins, live location, and friendships. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
