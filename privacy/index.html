Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

What Landed does

Landed is made by Muochu Hu (“we”). It lets you share your trips with friends you choose: flights, the train, car, or bus ride from the airport, and your hotel check-ins.

What we collect

Your email address (to sign in) and, if you continue with Google or Apple, the name and email they share with us; your name, username, and optional home airport; the trips you add (flight numbers, airports, times, places, hotels); hotel check-ins; and, only while you turn on live sharing for a ride, your device location.

Who can see it

Only people you accept as friends can see your trips, check-ins, and live location. Anyone signed in can look you up by your exact username to send a friend request; they see only your name and username.

Location

Landed asks for location only when you tap “Share live location” on a ride. We keep only your latest position, it is visible to your friends only, and it is deleted when you stop sharing, mark the ride arrived, or check in to a hotel. Landed does not track you in the background.

Services we use

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, they confirm who you are; we do not get your password or access to your Google or Apple account. Flight times may be looked up with a flight data provider using the flight number and date only. Place and hotel search use OpenStreetMap data (Photon and Overpass); the search text and map area are sent, not your identity. Maps on the web use OpenFreeMap tiles; on iPhone, Apple Maps.

No ads, no selling

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete any trip at any time. Profile > Delete account permanently deletes your account, trips, check-ins, live location, and friendships.

Contact

Questions, or a request to access or delete your data: email support@justlanded.app.
